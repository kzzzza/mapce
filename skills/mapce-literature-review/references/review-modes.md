# Review Modes

## Rapid review

Use for an initial map of a field, research planning, or an internal related-work note.
Record the question, sources, exact queries, search date, constraints, candidate list,
selection rationale, evidence table, thematic synthesis, gaps, and limitations.

Rapid review does not imply exhaustive coverage, dual screening, risk-of-bias
certification, or PRISMA compliance.

## PRISMA-style review

Before searching, record a protocol with review questions, databases, query plan,
date and language limits, inclusion/exclusion criteria, screening stages, extraction
fields, quality method, and planned synthesis.

Maintain counts for identified records, duplicates removed, title/abstract screening,
full-text assessment, exclusions by reason, and final inclusion. Do not invent dual
reviewers, inter-rater agreement, protocol registration, or quality ratings.

Select a quality tool that matches the study type. For CS/AI benchmarks, assess at
least dataset/task representativeness, baseline fairness, leakage, metric validity,
replication, uncertainty, compute reporting, code/data availability, and external validity.

Meta-analysis is outside this bundle unless the user supplies suitable numeric data
and an analysis method. Do not aggregate incompatible metrics into a pooled estimate.
