The MAPCE research skills are an original implementation informed by the
following public skill documents:

  <https://github.com/K-Dense-AI/scientific-agent-skills/blob/main/skills/scientific-writing/SKILL.md>

- K-Dense AI, `literature-review`, MIT license:

  <https://github.com/brycewang-stanford/Auto-Empirical-Research-Skills/blob/main/skills/43-wentorai-research-plugins/skills/writing/composition/research-paper-writer/SKILL.md>

The K-Dense workflows informed the separation of discovery, evidence,
verification, drafting, and human approval. MAPCE-specific instructions,
workspace schemas, scripts, templates, and wording were written for this
project. No text or template from `research-paper-writer` is copied.
